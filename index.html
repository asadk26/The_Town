Quiet Stacks

The library

One short shift on the desk. Returns pile into a cart and patrons queue up, and both are answered the same way — by pointing at a shelf — so you can only ever attend to one of them at a time.

Order Up

The lunch counter

A customer orders, the counter slides away, and you rebuild the ticket at the pass from memory. Every five tickets the orders grow by an item. Get one wrong and you can always go back and ask.

Mail Run

The postal round

One short route, five kerbside boxes. Steer, brake, line the truck up and hold it still to post the mail. Stop square and centred for a Perfect; overshoot and the brake becomes reverse.
